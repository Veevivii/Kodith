About

Kodith is a community for people who like building things with computers and hardware — students, self-taught tinkerers, and anyone curious enough to open the box and look inside.

We run online most of the time, so you can take part from anywhere, and we get together in person every so often to build in the same room. There is no application and nothing to pay.

What we do

Four things, done regularly.

  • Workshops

    Hands-on sessions where everyone builds the same thing at the same time.

  • Projects

    Small groups building real things together, in the open.

  • Meetups

    Occasional in-person build nights. Bring what you are working on.

  • Resources

    Notes, recordings and reading lists you can actually find again.
